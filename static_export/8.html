Introduction

The Indian Express UPSC Key series often combines multiple current-affairs themes relevant to aspirants. The March 13, 2025 edition analyzed three critical issues: the World Air Quality Report 2024, the Baloch insurgency in Pakistan, and India’s proposed Immigration and Foreigners Bill 2025. Together, these highlight the interconnections between environment, security, and governance. ________________________________________ World Air Quality Report 2024 • Findings: India continued to feature among the world’s worst performers, with 13 of the 20 most polluted cities globally located in the country. PM2.5 concentrations were nearly 10–12 times higher than WHO standards. • Health Impact: Air pollution is now the second largest risk factor for premature deaths in India, after malnutrition. Lancet (2022) estimated ~1.6 million deaths in India annually from air pollution. • Policy Gaps: Despite NCAP (National Clean Air Programme, 2019), implementation is uneven; many cities failed to achieve the 20–30% reduction target for PM2.5. • Global Diplomacy: India faces pressure to balance development with climate responsibility. ________________________________________ Baloch Insurgency • Context: Balochistan, Pakistan’s largest but least developed province, has witnessed insurgencies since the 1940s. • Current Trends: o Militants target security forces and Chinese assets linked to the China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC). o Increased attacks in Gwadar and Quetta regions during 2024–25. • Implications for India: o India officially denies involvement but faces accusations from Pakistan. o Instability in Balochistan threatens regional connectivity and raises concerns for India’s own security. • International Dimension: China’s growing stakes make Balochistan a site of great-power contestation. ________________________________________ Immigration & Foreigners Bill 2025 • Objective: Streamline visa, deportation, and refugee regulation by replacing multiple fragmented laws (like Foreigners Act 1946). • Key Provisions: o Stricter deportation rules. o Digitization of immigration records. o Centralized authority for refugee status determination. • Concerns: o Civil liberties activists warn of possible human rights violations, especially for vulnerable groups (refugees from Afghanistan, Myanmar, Bangladesh). o Fear of overlap with Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA) and NRC debates. ________________________________________ UPSC Relevance • GS-III (Environment): Air pollution, NCAP, global climate diplomacy. • GS-II (Governance & IR): Immigration policies; neighborhood stability. • GS-II (Security): Insurgency in Balochistan, regional instability. • Essay: “Environmental sustainability and human security are deeply interconnected.” ________________________________________

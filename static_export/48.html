Economic Times | Date: 2024 • ~600 words

Introduction

Fiscal policy is central to India’s economic governance. In 2024, Economic Times highlighted debates on fiscal deficit, public debt, and sustainability as India sought to balance high growth with macroeconomic stability. With general elections approaching, questions arose on whether populist spending could derail fiscal prudence. Conclusion

India’s fiscal position in 2024 reflects a delicate balancing act — growth requires spending, but sustainability demands prudence. The solution lies in strengthening revenues, targeting subsidies, and ensuring productive capex. For UPSC aspirants, fiscal deficit debates illustrate how economics, governance, and politics intersect in shaping policy choices.

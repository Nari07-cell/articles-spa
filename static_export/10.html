Budget 2024 — How India Trumped Global Woes to Emerge Victorious

Economic Times | Date: Feb 1, 2024 • ~600 words

Introduction

The Union Budget 2024 was unveiled against a backdrop of global headwinds: inflationary pressures, volatile energy prices due to the Russia–Ukraine war, and tightening monetary policies in advanced economies. Despite these challenges, India’s budget was widely praised as a balanced, growth-oriented, and resilient financial plan. Conclusion

Budget 2024 was more than a fiscal statement; it was a message to the world that India remains resilient in turbulent times.

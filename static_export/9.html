Exclusive Interview: Narendra Modi and the Unstoppable Rise of India

Newsweek | Date: Apr 10, 2024 • ~1200 words

Introduction

In April 2024, Newsweek carried an exclusive interview with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, framing him as the face of India’s “unstoppable rise.” The article sought to capture India’s transformation into a confident global power under Modi’s leadership — from economic reforms and digital transformation to assertive foreign policy. While the interview projected a bold image of India’s ambitions, it also generated debate on the gaps between narrative and reality.

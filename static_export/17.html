India’s Semiconductor Mission — Building Strategic Tech Sovereignty

Economic Times | Date: 2024 • ~600 words

Introduction

In 2024, India accelerated its efforts under the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) to develop domestic capacity in chip manufacturing. The global semiconductor shortage during COVID-19 and supply chain disruptions from geopolitical tensions highlighted the vulnerability of nations dependent on imports. 1. Public–Private Partnerships: Encourage global firms with Indian equity stakes.

Conclusion

Conclusion

The India Semiconductor Mission is ambitious but essential. While India may not rival Taiwan or South Korea in advanced nodes soon, building a domestic ecosystem for chip design, packaging, and specialty fabs is within reach. Success will secure India’s place in the global technology order and reduce vulnerability in times of geopolitical uncertainty. For UPSC aspirants, this topic demonstrates how economy, technology, and security converge in policy-making.

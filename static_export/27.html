India’s Federalism Test — Centre–State Fiscal Relations in 2024

The Hindu | Date: 2024 • ~600 words

Introduction

India’s cooperative federalism has often been tested by fiscal issues. In 2024, The Hindu highlighted growing tensions between the Centre and States over tax devolution, GST compensation, and centrally sponsored schemes. Conclusion

India’s federalism in 2024 is being stress-tested by fiscal disputes. For cooperative federalism to succeed, the Centre must ensure timely transfers and empower states with real autonomy, while states must demonstrate fiscal discipline. For UPSC aspirants, this issue exemplifies how polity, economy, and governance intersect in Centre–State dynamics.
